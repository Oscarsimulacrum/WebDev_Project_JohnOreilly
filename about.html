Welcome! I’m John O'Reilly, the founder of Wheels On Fire. I started this journey with a simple mission: to help car enthusiasts buy modified and upgraded cars without the usual hassle. With over 8 years of experience in car modification, I noticed a major gap in how modified cars were being handled. I knew there had to be a better, more efficient way. At Wheels On Fire, we believe in transparency, innovation, and delivering exceptional results. Whether you are looking to buy your first modified car or find your style, I am here to guide you every step of the way. When I’m not working on growing the business, you can usually find me drinking too much coffee and attending automotive conventions.
